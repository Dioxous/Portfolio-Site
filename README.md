Viewports

The viewports used were (max-width: 480px), (min-width: 481px) and (max-width: 960px), and (min-width: 960px).

These viewports were used as they are common viewports for their respective devices. \<480px  is common for smartphones, \>480px and \<960px is common for tablets, and \>960px is common for laptops and desktops.

Gradients

A linear gradient of dark blue to sand was used in the home page, index.html, under the #home-wrapper selector in the css file. An angle gradient of cream to deep blue was used in the profile picture background on the about page, about.html. The code is under the #profile-image img selector in the css file.

Colour Scheme

Colour scheme used: \#112140 (Dark blue), \#254159 (Deep blue), \#A67F5D (Sand), \#D9B18F (Cream), \#F2F2F2 (Off-white)

This colour scheme was used as it gives the site a modern look while looking simplistic. There's five colours but two of them are shades of each other, which helps to keep things more minimalistic and easy to look at. Overall, the scheme gives a premium and clean feel to the site.
